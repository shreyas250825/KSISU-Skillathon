02
The Problem

A clearer view of Karnataka's skill ecosystem

Three kinds of evidence shape skill planning, yet they come from different periods, geographies, and units — making them impossible to compare directly without a transparent integration layer.

Central Problem: Karnataka lacks a transparent, reproducible way to bring training, workforce and economic evidence together by district, sector and period. Without it, possible mismatches between training and the economy are hard to examine, and fragmented data invites over-interpretation.

Methodology

Six transparent indicators — what each informs and what it cannot establish

No black-box scores. Every indicator cites its formula, data source, and the questions it cannot answer. No composite SkillGap Index is claimed.

Pipeline

Solid green: Implemented in this prototype. Dashed terracotta: Proposed for production. Geo-temporal harmonisation (Step 5) and Sector/Trade Mapping (Step 6) require a versioned district-code crosswalk and expert-reviewed trade→sector map.

Indicators

No composite index. Any composite would combine I5-type components and must be validated by domain experts before use. No such validation exists here. Flags are prompts for investigation, not proofs of shortage.

Policy Insights & Next Steps

What officials can investigate with this system

Observed patterns (from data)

Training Volume
GTTC reported counts grew overall — but aggregate totals hide centre swings
1,040 → 2,515 → 2,086 → 2,420 (total 8,061). The like-for-like cohort of 3 centres fell 52.9% in 2020–21, but the aggregate fell only 17.1% as 3 new centres began reporting. Mysuru holds 34.6%; top two centres hold 59.7%.
Source: GTTC_Shortterm_Courses.csv · Verified by team calculation
Historical Workforce
Agriculture, forestry & fishing dominated marginal work in Karnataka (2011)
~63.7% of Census 2011 marginal worker counts fall in agricultural categories. Female share among marginal workers is higher than male share (~57% vs ~43%).
Source: Census 2011 B-06 · Marginal workers only, 2011 boundaries
Economic Signal
SLSWCC approvals concentrated in Drugs/Chemicals, Engineering, Food
307 approved units; top sectors by listed employment are Drugs/Chemicals, Engineering, Food & Agribusiness. 8 units listed zero employment.
Source: SLSWCC 2021 · Listed employment ≠ realised jobs

Still to validate (gaps and next steps)

No demand data linked yet. None of the available files contains employer vacancy data, occupational demand forecasts, or placement outcome records. An alignment indicator cannot be computed without a compatible economic demand series.
Count definitions unverified. The GTTC short-term file's count unit is not defined in a data dictionary. Its totals equal the "CoE" row of the passed-out file, suggesting both files are from one source — not two independent verifications.
District crosswalks missing. Census 2011 uses 30 districts with 2011 boundaries. Later files use different naming conventions. Bangalore split into multiple districts; Belgaum became Belagavi. A versioned code crosswalk is required.
Recommended next steps
Phase 2: Additional datasets needed
ITI enrolment and trades data · Udyam/MSME registrations · Employer survey or vacancy data · Portal MIS module · Jobs module · Verified count definitions from GTTC · District code crosswalk
